# econ227project

**Question:**

Using data regarding contemporary developed countries in the past, such as constituent states of the European Union, to what extent does pollution level change as countries industrialize and income level increases?

**Description:**

In this research project, we will look at what happens to greenhouse gas emissions as a country industrializes and what happens after.

To do this, we will examine the relationship between GDP per capita and different metrics such as per capita CO₂, methane, and nitrous oxide emissions.

Our topic connects to topic 3 of the course, regarding poverty, inequality, economic growth, allocations. In particular, we will examine how emissions change as economies grow.
